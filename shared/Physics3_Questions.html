PART 2Answer Key and Worked Solutions

Set 1 · Conservation of Energy (Week 1)

Q1  Answer: C  (400 J)

PE at top = mgh = 2 × 10 × 20 = 400 J. All of it becomes KE at the ground (check: v = √(2gh) = 20 m s⁻¹, ½mv² = ½ × 2 × 400 = 400 J).

Trap: 200 J comes from ½mgh; 800 J from doubling; 40 J from m × h.

Q2  Answer: B  (20 m)

KE at launch = ½ × 0.5 × 20² = 100 J. At the top KE = 0, so mgh = 100: h = 100 / (0.5 × 10) = 20 m. Shortcut: h = v²/2g = 400/20.

Trap: 40 m comes from v²/g; mass cancels, so it never affects the height.

Q3  Answer: D  (20 N)

PE lost = 4 × 10 × 5 = 200 J. The track is smooth, so the block reaches the bottom with 200 J of KE. Friction does all of it away as heat: F × 10 = 200, so F = 20 N.

Trap: Two stages: the curved track wastes nothing, the rough floor wastes everything. 40 N comes from using 5 m, not 10 m, as the stopping distance.

Set 2 · Electrical Energy, Heat and Efficiency (Week 1)

Q4  Answer: A  (4.17 A and 9.0 × 10⁵ J)

I = P/V = 1 000/240 = 4.17 A. E = Pt = 1 000 × (15 × 60) = 9.0 × 10⁵ J.

Trap: B forgets to convert minutes to seconds. C inverts P/V.

Q5  Answer: D  (300 s)

Heat needed Q = mcΔθ = 1.5 × 4 200 × 80 = 504 000 J. Useful power = 0.84 × 2 000 = 1 680 W. t = 504 000 / 1 680 = 300 s.

Trap: 252 s ignores the efficiency (504 000 / 2 000). 357 s divides by 0.84 on the wrong side of the equation.

Q6  Answer: C  (1.35 kWh)

Power falling on panel = 2 × 1 000 = 2 000 W. Electrical output = 0.15 × 2 000 = 300 W. Into battery = 0.90 × 300 = 270 W. Energy = 270 W × 5 h = 1 350 Wh = 1.35 kWh.

Trap: Two efficiencies in a chain are multiplied (0.15 × 0.90 = 0.135 overall). 1.50 kWh drops the battery stage; 10 kWh drops both.

Set 3 · Gears and Belt Drives (Week 2)

Q7  Answer: C  (600 rpm)

N₁T₁ = N₂T₂, so N₂ = 300 × 40 / 20 = 600 rpm. Fewer teeth on the driven gear means it turns faster.

Trap: 150 rpm inverts the ratio.

Q8  Answer: D  (500 rpm and 0.4)

N₂ = N₁D₁/D₂ = 200 × 30 / 12 = 500 rpm. VR = N₁/N₂ = 200/500 = 0.4 (= D₂/D₁ = 12/30). VR below 1 means speed is increased.

Trap: 80 rpm and 2.5 come from inverting the diameters.

Q9  Answer: B  (100 rpm and 32.4 N m)

Stage ratios: B/A = 60/20 = 3; D/C = 45/15 = 3. Overall reduction = 3 × 3 = 9. N_D = 900/9 = 100 rpm. Ideal torque = 4 × 9 = 36 N m. Real torque = 0.90 × 36 = 32.4 N m.

Trap: 36 N m is the ideal (100%) torque. B and C turn at one speed because they share a shaft.

Set 4 · Transformers and Power Lines (Week 2)

Q10  Answer: B  (12 V)

Vs/Vp = Ns/Np, so Vs = 240 × 50/1 000 = 12 V.

Trap: 4 800 V inverts the turns ratio.

Q11  Answer: A  (90 A)

Power in = 240 × 5 = 1 200 W. Power out = 0.90 × 1 200 = 1 080 W. Is = 1 080/12 = 90 A.

Trap: 100 A is the ideal-transformer answer (1 200/12) with the loss ignored.

Q12  Answer: D  (50 kW to 500 W)

At 1 000 V: I = P/V = 100 000/1 000 = 100 A; loss = I²R = 100² × 5 = 50 000 W. At 10 000 V: I = 10 A; loss = 10² × 5 = 500 W. Voltage × 10 gives current ÷ 10 and loss ÷ 100.

Trap: A forgets that loss depends on I², so it divides by 10 instead of 100.

Set 5 · Mechanical Advantage, Velocity Ratio and Efficiency (Week 3)

Q13  Answer: C  (80%)

MA = L/E = 600/150 = 4. η = MA/VR × 100 = 4/5 × 100 = 80%.

Trap: 125% would mean energy is created; efficiency can never exceed 100%.

Q14  Answer: C  (150 N)

MA = η × VR = 0.75 × 8 = 6. E = L/MA = 900/6 = 150 N.

Trap: 112.5 N (900/8) is the effort for an ideal machine.

Q15  Answer: B  (90% and 300 J)

Work in = 250 × 12 = 3 000 J. Work out = 900 × 3 = 2 700 J. η = 2 700/3 000 = 90%. Wasted = 3 000 − 2 700 = 300 J. (Check: VR = 12/3 = 4, MA = 900/250 = 3.6, 3.6/4 = 90%.)

Trap: 2 700 J is the useful work, not the waste. 111% comes from dividing input by output.

Set 6 · Levers, Wheel and Axle (Week 3)

Q16  Answer: A  (200 N)

Moments: E × 2 = L × 0.5, so L = 50 × 2/0.5 = 200 N. (VR = 2/0.5 = 4, so MA = 4 for the ideal lever.)

Trap: 12.5 N inverts the arms.

Q17  Answer: D  (83.3%)

VR = R/r = 40/5 = 8. MA = 400/60 = 6.67. η = 6.67/8 × 100 = 83.3%.

Trap: 120% is VR ÷ MA (upside down); 15% is E ÷ L, the reciprocal of MA.

Q18  Answer: C  (83.3 cm)

Anticlockwise: 5 N × 30 cm = 150 N cm. Rule's own weight acts at its centre, the 50 cm mark, 10 cm to the right of the pivot: clockwise 2 × 10 = 20 N cm. Remaining clockwise moment needed = 130 N cm, so 3 × d = 130, d = 43.3 cm to the right of 40 cm, at the 83.3 cm mark.

Trap: 90 cm comes from ignoring the weight of the rule (150/3 = 50 cm from the pivot).

Set 7 · Pulley Systems and Inclined Plane (Week 3)

Q19  Answer: B  (70%)

MA = 420/150 = 2.8. η = 2.8/4 × 100 = 70%.

Trap: 143% is VR ÷ MA, the wrong way round. Efficiency above 100% is impossible.

Q20  Answer: C  (250 N and 12 m)

MA = 0.60 × 6 = 3.6. E = 900/3.6 = 250 N. VR = d_E/d_L, so d_E = 6 × 2 = 12 m (VR is geometric, so friction does not change it).

Trap: 150 N is the ideal effort (900/6).

Q21  Answer: A  (75% and 100 N)

Work in = 400 × 5 = 2 000 J. Useful work = 1 000 × 1.5 = 1 500 J. η = 75%. Wasted = 500 J = f × 5, so f = 100 N. (Check: E = L sinθ + f = 1 000 × 0.3 + f = 400, so f = 100 N.)

Trap: 500 N is the wasted energy in joules, not the force; divide by the 5 m.

Set 8 · Hydraulic Press (Week 3)

Q22  Answer: B  (4 000 N)

Pressure = 100/5 = 20 N cm⁻². Load = 20 × 200 = 4 000 N. (VR = A/a = 200/5 = 40.)

Trap: Find the area ratio first: 200/5 = 40, so the load must be 40 times the effort.

Q23  Answer: A  (1 800 N)

VR = A/a = (R/r)² = (10/2)² = 25. MA = 0.90 × 25 = 22.5. L = 22.5 × 80 = 1 800 N.

Trap: 400 N uses the radius ratio (5) instead of the area ratio (25). 2 000 N is the ideal load.

Q24  Answer: C  (2 400 N and 0.2 cm)

VR = 100/2 = 50. MA = 0.80 × 50 = 40. Load = 40 × 60 = 2 400 N. Distance: d_L = d_E/VR = 10/50 = 0.2 cm (liquid volume moved is the same: 2 × 10 = 100 × d_L).

Trap: 3 000 N is the ideal load (50 × 60). 1 920 N applies the efficiency twice; 0.25 cm divides the distance by it. Efficiency changes the force, not the distance.

Set 9 · Hydroelectric Power (Week 6)

Q25  Answer: C  (1.0 × 10⁷ W)

P = ρQgh = 1 000 × 20 × 10 × 50 = 1.0 × 10⁷ W (10 MW).

Trap: Mass flow rate is ρQ = 20 000 kg s⁻¹, not 20.

Q26  Answer: A  (9.6 MW)

P available = 1 000 × 30 × 10 × 40 = 12 × 10⁶ W = 12 MW. Output = 0.80 × 12 = 9.6 MW.

Trap: 12 MW is the input power; 15 MW divides by 0.8.

Q27  Answer: D  (20.0 m³ s⁻¹)

Overall η = 0.90 × 0.95 = 0.855. P_out = 0.855 × ρQgh, so Q = 10.26 × 10⁶ / (0.855 × 1 000 × 10 × 60) = 10.26 × 10⁶ / 513 000 = 20 m³ s⁻¹.

Trap: Efficiencies of stages in series multiply. 17.1 m³ s⁻¹ multiplies Q by 0.855 instead of dividing.

Set 10 · Rocket Thrust and Acceleration (Week 8)

Q28  Answer: B  (1.0 × 10⁵ N)

Thrust = (dm/dt) × v_e = 50 × 2 000 = 1.0 × 10⁵ N.

Trap: 40 N is 2 000/50, dividing instead of multiplying.

Q29  Answer: C  (10 m s⁻²)

Net force = thrust − weight = 30 000 − (1 500 × 10) = 15 000 N. a = 15 000/1 500 = 10 m s⁻².

Trap: 20 m s⁻² is thrust ÷ mass with the weight forgotten.

Q30  Answer: D  (120 000 N and 5 m s⁻²)

Thrust = 40 × 3 000 = 120 000 N. Weight = 8 000 × 10 = 80 000 N. a = (120 000 − 80 000)/8 000 = 5 m s⁻². As fuel burns the mass falls, so the acceleration rises.

Trap: 15 m s⁻² is thrust ÷ mass with the weight forgotten; 40 000 N is the net force, not the thrust.
