Part I — How to Use This Supplement

Study Protocol
  1. Read every notation display aloud as a full mathematical sentence. "3 over 4" for 34, "integral from 0 to 2 of x dx" for the definite integral, "5 choose 2" for the combination.
  2. Redraw each notation format from memory. Especially: matrix brackets, fraction bars, integral limits, sigma notation, system braces.
  3. Practise reading tables under time pressure. JAMB gives you roughly 90 seconds per question. Table-based statistics questions must be solved in under 2 minutes.
  4. Keep the symbol reference table (Part F) beside you during practice. Every symbol should become instant recall.
  5. Complete the 25 notation questions and score 22+ before moving to the next topic.
