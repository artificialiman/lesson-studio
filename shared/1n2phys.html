APower and Energy Unit Conversions

Question 1

Convert 5 000 000 W to MW.

Solution 
1 MW = 1 000 000 W = 10⁶ W
5 000 000 W = 5 000 000 / 1 000 000 = 5 MW

Answer: 5 MW.

Question 2

Convert 2.4 MW to W.

Solution 
2.4 MW = 2.4 × 1 000 000 W
        = 2 400 000 W = 2.4 × 10⁶ W

Answer: 2.4 × 10⁶ W.

Question 3

Convert 750 kW to MW.

Solution 
1 kW = 1 000 W
1 MW = 1 000 kW
750 kW = 750 / 1 000 = 0.75 MW

Answer: 0.75 MW.

Question 4

Convert 500 W to kW and to MW.

Solution 
500 W = 500 / 1 000 kW = 0.5 kW
500 W = 500 / 1 000 000 MW = 5 × 10⁻⁴ MW

Answer: 0.5 kW; 5 × 10⁻⁴ MW.

Question 5

Convert 3 kWh to joules.

Solution 
1 kWh = 1 000 W × 3 600 s = 3.6 × 10⁶ J
3 kWh = 3 × 3.6 × 10⁶ = 1.08 × 10⁷ J

Answer: 1.08 × 10⁷ J.

Question 6

Convert 7.2 × 10⁶ J to kWh.

Solution 
1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J
7.2 × 10⁶ J = 7.2 × 10⁶ / 3.6 × 10⁶ = 2 kWh

Answer: 2 kWh.

Question 7

A 2 MW generator runs for 5 hours. Find the energy produced in kWh and in joules.

Solution 
P = 2 MW = 2 000 kW
Energy = P × t = 2 000 kW × 5 h = 10 000 kWh
In joules: 10 000 × 3.6 × 10⁶ = 3.6 × 10¹⁰ J

Answer: 10 000 kWh = 3.6 × 10¹⁰ J.

Question 8

Convert 15 mA to A.

Solution 
1 mA = 10⁻³ A
15 mA = 15 × 10⁻³ = 1.5 × 10⁻² A = 0.015 A

Answer: 0.015 A.

Question 9

Convert 220 μA to mA and to A.

Solution 
1 μA = 10⁻⁶ A
220 μA = 220 × 10⁻⁶ A = 2.2 × 10⁻⁴ A
In mA: 2.2 × 10⁻⁴ / 10⁻³ = 0.22 mA

Answer: 0.22 mA; 2.2 × 10⁻⁴ A.

Question 10

Convert 4 500 J to kJ and to MJ.

Solution 
4 500 J = 4 500 / 1 000 = 4.5 kJ
4 500 J = 4 500 / 1 000 000 = 4.5 × 10⁻³ MJ

Answer: 4.5 kJ; 4.5 × 10⁻³ MJ.

Question 11

A household uses 450 kWh of electricity in a month. How many joules is this?

Solution 
450 kWh = 450 × 3.6 × 10⁶
         = 1.62 × 10⁹ J

Answer: 1.62 × 10⁹ J.

Question 12

A power station delivers 132 MW. Express this in kW and in W.

Solution 
132 MW = 132 × 1 000 kW = 132 000 kW
132 MW = 132 × 10⁶ W = 1.32 × 10⁸ W

Answer: 132 000 kW = 1.32 × 10⁸ W.

BSpeed, Distance and Time Conversions

Question 13

Convert 72 km/h to m/s.

Solution 
72 km/h = 72 × 1 000 m / 3 600 s
        = 72 000 / 3 600 = 20 m/s

Answer: 20 m/s.

Question 14

Convert 25 m/s to km/h.

Solution 
25 m/s = 25 × 3 600 / 1 000 km/h
       = 90 000 / 1 000 = 90 km/h

Answer: 90 km/h.

Question 15

A car travels 180 km in 2 hours 30 minutes. Find its average speed in km/h and in m/s.

Solution 
t = 2.5 h
v = 180 / 2.5 = 72 km/h
In m/s: 72 × 1000/3600 = 20 m/s

Answer: 72 km/h = 20 m/s.

Question 16

How long does it take a car moving at 90 km/h to cover 225 km? Give the answer in hours and minutes.

Solution 
t = d / v = 225 / 90 = 2.5 h
0.5 h = 30 min
t = 2 h 30 min

Answer: 2 hours 30 minutes.

Question 17

A body moves at 40 m/s for 30 s. Find the distance covered in m and in km.

Solution 
d = v × t = 40 × 30 = 1 200 m
In km: 1 200 / 1 000 = 1.2 km

Answer: 1 200 m = 1.2 km.

Question 18

Convert a speed of 108 km/h to m/s.

Solution 
108 km/h = 108 × 1000 / 3600
         = 108 000 / 3 600 = 30 m/s

Answer: 30 m/s.

Question 19

A runner covers 800 m in 2 minutes. Find the average speed in m/s.

Solution 
t = 2 × 60 = 120 s
v = 800 / 120 = 6.67 m/s

Answer: ≈ 6.67 m/s.

Question 20

A car travels 45 m in 3 s. Find its speed in (a) m/s and (b) km/h.

Solution 
(a) v = 45 / 3 = 15 m/s
(b) 15 × 3 600 / 1 000 = 54 km/h

Answer: (a) 15 m/s; (b) 54 km/h.

Question 21

A body moves at 0.5 km/min. Express this in m/s.

Solution 
0.5 km/min = 500 m / 60 s
           = 8.33 m/s

Question 22

Convert 15 cm/s to m/s and to km/h.

Solution 
15 cm/s = 0.15 m/s
In km/h: 0.15 × 3600 / 1000 = 0.54 km/h

Answer: 0.15 m/s = 0.54 km/h.

Question 23

A plane flies 1 500 km in 2 h. Find its speed in m/s.

Solution 
v = 1 500 / 2 = 750 km/h
In m/s: 750 × 1000 / 3600 = 208.3 m/s

Answer: ≈ 208.3 m/s.

Question 24

A body moves at 20 m/s for 15 minutes. Find the distance in km.

Solution 
t = 15 × 60 = 900 s
d = 20 × 900 = 18 000 m = 18 km

CLength, Area, Volume and Density Conversions

Question 25

Convert 3.5 m to cm and to mm.

Solution 
3.5 m = 3.5 × 100 cm = 350 cm
3.5 m = 3.5 × 1000 mm = 3 500 mm

Answer: 350 cm; 3 500 mm.

Question 26

Convert 2 500 cm to m and to km.

Solution 
2 500 cm = 2 500 / 100 = 25 m
25 m = 25 / 1 000 = 0.025 km

Answer: 25 m = 0.025 km.

Question 27

Convert 5 m² to cm².

Solution 
1 m = 100 cm, so 1 m² = 100² = 10 000 cm²
5 m² = 5 × 10 000 = 50 000 cm²

Answer: 50 000 cm².

Question 28

Convert 4 m³ to cm³ and to litres.

Solution 
1 m³ = 100³ cm³ = 10⁶ cm³
4 m³ = 4 × 10⁶ cm³
1 m³ = 1 000 L
4 m³ = 4 000 L

Answer: 4 × 10⁶ cm³ = 4 000 litres.

Question 29

Convert 250 cm³ to m³.

Solution 
1 cm³ = 10⁻⁶ m³
250 cm³ = 250 × 10⁻⁶ = 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ m³

Answer: 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ m³.

Question 30

Convert a density of 2.7 g/cm³ to kg/m³.

Solution 
1 g/cm³ = 1 000 kg/m³
2.7 g/cm³ = 2.7 × 1 000 = 2 700 kg/m³

Answer: 2 700 kg/m³.

Question 31

Convert a density of 800 kg/m³ to g/cm³.

Solution 
1 kg/m³ = 10⁻³ g/cm³
800 kg/m³ = 800 × 10⁻³ = 0.8 g/cm³

Answer: 0.8 g/cm³.

Question 32

A block has a mass of 450 g and a volume of 150 cm³. Find its density in g/cm³ and in kg/m³.

Solution 
ρ = 450 / 150 = 3 g/cm³
In kg/m³: 3 × 1 000 = 3 000 kg/m³

Answer: 3 g/cm³ = 3 000 kg/m³.

Question 33

Convert 2.5 g to kg and to mg.

Solution 
2.5 g = 2.5 × 10⁻³ kg = 0.0025 kg
2.5 g = 2.5 × 1 000 mg = 2 500 mg

Answer: 0.0025 kg; 2 500 mg.

Question 34

A tank measures 2 m × 1.5 m × 0.8 m. Find its volume in m³ and in litres.

Solution 
V = 2 × 1.5 × 0.8 = 2.4 m³
In litres: 2.4 × 1 000 = 2 400 L

Answer: 2.4 m³ = 2 400 litres.

Question 35

The density of water is 1 000 kg/m³. Find the mass of 0.5 m³ of water in kg and in tonnes.

Solution 
m = ρV = 1 000 × 0.5 = 500 kg
In tonnes: 500 / 1 000 = 0.5 tonnes

Answer: 500 kg = 0.5 tonnes.

Question 36

Convert 72 km/h to m/s and then compute the distance travelled in 25 s.

Solution 
72 km/h = 20 m/s
d = v × t = 20 × 25 = 500 m

Answer: 500 m.

DForce, Weight and Pressure Conversions

Question 37

Find the weight of a 20 kg mass. (g = 10 m/s²)

Solution 
W = mg = 20 × 10 = 200 N

Answer: 200 N.

Question 38

A force of 500 N acts on an area of 0.25 m². Find the pressure in Pa and in kPa.

Solution 
P = F/A = 500 / 0.25 = 2 000 Pa
In kPa: 2 000 / 1 000 = 2 kPa

Answer: 2 000 Pa = 2 kPa.

Question 39

A pressure of 4 × 10⁵ Pa acts on an area of 50 cm². Find the force in N.

Solution 
A = 50 cm² = 50 × 10⁻⁴ m² = 5 × 10⁻³ m²
F = P × A = 4 × 10⁵ × 5 × 10⁻³ = 2 000 N

Answer: 2 000 N.

Question 40

Convert 1.5 kN to N and to MN.

Solution 
1.5 kN = 1.5 × 1 000 = 1 500 N
1.5 kN = 1.5 × 10⁻³ MN = 0.0015 MN

Answer: 1 500 N = 0.0015 MN.

Question 41

Convert 0.5 MJ to J and to kJ.

Solution 
0.5 MJ = 0.5 × 10⁶ = 5 × 10⁵ J
5 × 10⁵ J = 5 × 10⁵ / 1 000 = 500 kJ

Answer: 5 × 10⁵ J = 500 kJ.

Question 42

A pump raises 300 kg of water through 15 m in 30 s. Find the power in W and kW. (g = 10 m/s²)

Solution 
W = mgh = 300 × 10 × 15 = 45 000 J
P = 45 000 / 30 = 1 500 W = 1.5 kW

Answer: 1 500 W = 1.5 kW.

Question 43

A car of mass 1 200 kg accelerates at 2.5 m/s². Find the force on it in N and in kN.

Solution 
F = ma = 1 200 × 2.5 = 3 000 N
3 000 N = 3 kN

Answer: 3 000 N = 3 kN.

Question 44

A hydraulic press has pistons of area 5 cm² and 300 cm². If a force of 40 N is applied to the small piston, find the force on the large piston.

Solution 
F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂
40/5 = F₂/300
F₂ = (40 × 300)/5 = 2 400 N = 2.4 kN

Answer: 2 400 N = 2.4 kN.

EEquations of Motion Computations

Question 45

A car accelerates from rest at 4 m/s² for 8 s. Find its final velocity in m/s and km/h.

Solution 
v = u + at = 0 + 4 × 8 = 32 m/s
In km/h: 32 × 3600/1000 = 115.2 km/h

Answer: 32 m/s = 115.2 km/h.

Question 46

A car travelling at 25 m/s brakes uniformly and stops in 5 s. Find the retardation in m/s² and the distance in m.

Solution 
a = (0 − 25)/5 = −5 m/s²  →  retardation = 5 m/s²
s = ((25 + 0)/2) × 5 = 62.5 m

Answer: 5 m/s²; 62.5 m.

Question 47

A car accelerates from 20 km/h to 80 km/h in 10 s. Find the acceleration in m/s².

Solution 
u = 20 km/h = 5.56 m/s
v = 80 km/h = 22.22 m/s
a = (22.22 − 5.56)/10 = 1.67 m/s²

Answer: ≈ 1.67 m/s².

Question 48

A body moving at 15 m/s decelerates at 3 m/s². How long until it stops? What distance does it cover in that time?

Solution 
0 = 15 − 3t  →  t = 5 s
s = ((15 + 0)/2) × 5 = 37.5 m

Answer: 5 s; 37.5 m.

Question 49

A stone is dropped from a height of 80 m. Find the time to fall and the velocity at impact. (g = 10 m/s²)

Solution 
h = ½gt²  →  80 = 5t²  →  t = 4 s
v = gt = 10 × 4 = 40 m/s

Answer: 4 s; 40 m/s.

Question 50

A ball is thrown vertically upwards at 30 m/s. Find the maximum height and the total time of flight. (g = 10 m/s²)

Solution 
0 = 30² − 2 × 10 × h  →  h = 900/20 = 45 m
Time up: t = 30/10 = 3 s
Total flight = 6 s

Answer: 45 m; 6 s.

Question 51

A body has initial velocity 10 m/s and accelerates at 2 m/s². Find (a) velocity after 12 s, (b) distance travelled.

Solution 
(a) v = 10 + 2 × 12 = 34 m/s
(b) s = 10 × 12 + ½ × 2 × 144
    = 120 + 144 = 264 m

Answer: (a) 34 m/s; (b) 264 m.

Question 52

A train moving at 20 m/s accelerates uniformly to 40 m/s in 15 s. Find (a) acceleration, (b) distance travelled.

Solution 
(a) a = (40 − 20)/15 = 1.33 m/s²
(b) s = ((20 + 40)/2) × 15 = 450 m

Answer: (a) 1.33 m/s²; (b) 450 m.

Question 53

A car travelling at 90 km/h slows to 54 km/h in 5 s. Find (a) the deceleration in m/s², (b) the distance travelled.

Solution 
u = 90 km/h = 25 m/s
v = 54 km/h = 15 m/s
(a) a = (15 − 25)/5 = −2 m/s²  →  deceleration = 2 m/s²
(b) s = ((25 + 15)/2) × 5 = 100 m

Answer: (a) 2 m/s²; (b) 100 m.

Question 54

A body is thrown vertically upwards at 50 m/s. Find (a) time to reach the top, (b) total flight time, (c) maximum height. (g = 10 m/s²)

Solution 
(a) t = 50/10 = 5 s
(b) total = 2 × 5 = 10 s
(c) h = 50²/(2 × 10) = 2500/20 = 125 m

Answer: 5 s; 10 s; 125 m.

Question 55

A stone is thrown vertically downwards from a height of 100 m at 5 m/s. Find its velocity on hitting the ground. (g = 10 m/s²)

Solution 
v² = u² + 2gh = 25 + 2 × 10 × 100
v² = 25 + 2000 = 2025
v = √2025 = 45 m/s

Answer: 45 m/s.

Question 56

A car covers 300 m while accelerating uniformly from 10 m/s to 30 m/s. Find (a) the acceleration, (b) the time taken.

Solution 
(a) v² = u² + 2as
    30² = 10² + 2a(300)
    900 = 100 + 600a
    600a = 800  →  a = 1.33 m/s²
(b) t = (30 − 10)/1.33 = 15 s

Answer: (a) 1.33 m/s²; (b) 15 s.

FDistance, Displacement and Vector Computations

Question 57

A boy walks 12 m east, then 5 m west. Find the distance and the displacement in m.

Solution 
Distance = 12 + 5 = 17 m
Displacement = 12 − 5 = 7 m east

Answer: 17 m; 7 m east.

Question 58

A girl walks 9 m north, then 12 m east. Find the magnitude of the displacement in m.

Solution 
d = √(9² + 12²) = √(81 + 144) = √225 = 15 m

Answer: 15 m.

Question 59

A car travels 40 km east and then 30 km north in 2 hours. Find (a) the distance, (b) the displacement, (c) the average speed in km/h.

Solution 
(a) Distance = 40 + 30 = 70 km
(b) Displacement = √(40² + 30²) = √2500 = 50 km
(c) Average speed = 70/2 = 35 km/h

Answer: 70 km; 50 km; 35 km/h.

Question 60

A runner completes one lap of a circular track of radius 50 m in 40 s. Find (a) the distance in m, (b) the displacement, (c) the average speed in m/s. (π = 3.142)

Solution 
(a) Distance = 2πr = 2 × 3.142 × 50 = 314.2 m
(b) Displacement = 0 (start = finish)
(c) Average speed = 314.2/40 = 7.86 m/s

Answer: 314.2 m; 0; 7.86 m/s.

Question 61

Two forces of 8 N and 15 N act at right angles. Find the magnitude of the resultant in N.

Solution 
R = √(8² + 15²) = √(64 + 225) = √289 = 17 N

Answer: 17 N.

Question 62

A force of 50 N acts at 37° to the horizontal. Find its horizontal and vertical components. (cos 37° = 0.8, sin 37° = 0.6)

Solution 
Horizontal = 50 cos 37° = 50 × 0.8 = 40 N
Vertical = 50 sin 37° = 50 × 0.6 = 30 N

Answer: 40 N; 30 N.

Question 63

Two forces of 6 N and 10 N act at 60° to each other. Find the resultant. (cos 60° = 0.5)

Solution 
R = √(6² + 10² + 2 × 6 × 10 × 0.5)
R = √(36 + 100 + 60) = √196 = 14 N

Answer: 14 N.

Question 64

A plane flies 400 km due north then 300 km due east. Find (a) the total distance, (b) the magnitude of displacement, (c) the direction of the displacement.

Solution 
(a) Distance = 400 + 300 = 700 km
(b) Displacement = √(400² + 300²) = √250000 = 500 km
(c) tan θ = 300/400 = 0.75
    θ = 36.9° east of north

Answer: 700 km; 500 km at 36.9° east of north.

GLight — Refraction Computations

Question 65

The speed of light in a medium is 2 × 10⁸ m/s. Find its refractive index. (c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s)

Solution 
n = c/v = 3 × 10⁸ / 2 × 10⁸ = 1.5

Answer: 1.5.

Question 66

The refractive index of water is 1.33. Find the speed of light in water in m/s and in km/s.

Solution 
v = c/n = 3 × 10⁸ / 1.33 = 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
In km/s: 2.26 × 10⁸ / 1 000 = 2.26 × 10⁵ km/s

Answer: 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s = 2.26 × 10⁵ km/s.

Question 67

Light passes from air into glass at 45° and refracts at 28°. Find the refractive index of the glass.

Solution 
n = sin i / sin r = sin 45° / sin 28°
  = 0.7071 / 0.4695 = 1.51

Answer: ≈ 1.51.

Question 68

Find the critical angle for a glass–air boundary. (n = 1.5)

Solution 
sin C = 1/n = 1/1.5 = 0.6667
C = sin⁻¹(0.6667) = 41.8°

Answer: 41.8°.

Question 69

Find the critical angle for a water–air boundary. (n = 1.33)

Solution 
sin C = 1/1.33 = 0.7519
C = sin⁻¹(0.7519) = 48.8°

Answer: 48.8°.

Question 70

A coin lies at the bottom of a pool 2.4 m deep. The refractive index of water is 1.33. Find the apparent depth in m and cm.

Solution 
apparent depth = real depth / n
= 2.4/1.33 = 1.80 m = 180 cm

Answer: 1.80 m = 180 cm.

Question 71

The refractive index of diamond is 2.42. Find its critical angle.

Solution 
sin C = 1/2.42 = 0.4132
C = sin⁻¹(0.4132) = 24.4°

Answer: 24.4°.

Question 72

A ray strikes a plane mirror at an angle of 40° to the surface. Find (a) the angle of incidence, (b) the angle of reflection.

Solution 
(a) i = 90° − 40° = 50°
(b) r = i = 50°

Answer: (a) 50°; (b) 50°.

HSound, Charges, Fields and Gravitation

Question 73

A sound wave of frequency 500 Hz travels at 340 m/s. Find its wavelength in m and cm.

Solution 
λ = v/f = 340/500 = 0.68 m = 68 cm

Answer: 0.68 m = 68 cm.

Question 74

A ship sends a pulse to the seabed and receives the echo after 2.4 s. If the speed of sound in seawater is 1 500 m/s, find the depth in m and km.

Solution 
d = v × t / 2 = 1 500 × 2.4 / 2
  = 1 800 m = 1.8 km

Answer: 1 800 m = 1.8 km.

Question 75

Two charges of +3 μC and +5 μC are 0.15 m apart. Find the force between them. (k = 9 × 10⁹ N m² C⁻²)

Solution 
F = k q₁q₂ / r²
= (9 × 10⁹)(3 × 10⁻⁶)(5 × 10⁻⁶) / (0.15)²
= (135 × 10⁻³) / 0.0225
= 6 N (repulsive)

Answer: 6 N, repulsive.

Question 76

Find the electric field intensity 0.4 m from a +6 μC charge in N/C and kN/C.

Solution 
E = kQ/r²
= (9 × 10⁹)(6 × 10⁻⁶) / (0.4)²
= (54 × 10³) / 0.16
= 3.375 × 10⁵ N/C
= 337.5 kN/C

Answer: 3.375 × 10⁵ N/C = 337.5 kN/C.

Question 77

Find the gravitational force between masses of 40 kg and 60 kg placed 2 m apart. (G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m² kg⁻²)

Solution 
F = G m₁m₂ / r²
= (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹)(40)(60) / 4
= (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹)(2400) / 4
= (16 008 × 10⁻¹¹) / 4
= 4.0 × 10⁻⁸ N

Answer: ≈ 4 × 10⁻⁸ N.

Question 78

A body has mass 60 kg. Find its weight on the Moon. (g = 1.6 m/s²)

Solution 
W = mg = 60 × 1.6 = 96 N

Answer: 96 N.

Question 79

A body falls freely from 20 m. Find its velocity on impact in m/s and km/h. (g = 10 m/s²)

Solution 
v = √(2gh) = √(2 × 10 × 20)
  = √400 = 20 m/s
In km/h: 20 × 3600/1000 = 72 km/h

Answer: 20 m/s = 72 km/h.

Question 80

A car of mass 800 kg moves at 72 km/h. Find its kinetic energy in J and kJ.

Solution 
v = 72 km/h = 20 m/s
KE = ½mv² = ½ × 800 × 20²
   = 400 × 400 = 160 000 J = 160 kJ

Answer: 160 000 J = 160 kJ.
