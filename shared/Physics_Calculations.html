PART 1How Every Physics Calculation Is Built

Each calculation family has one source relation. Every question in the family is that relation with one or more of six changes applied. Listing the changes for each family shows every form a question can take.

The Six Changes

ChangeWhat variesExample
Unknown swappedThe same relation solved for each quantity in turnv = u + at solved for v, for u, for a or for t
Indirect inputA quantity has to be built before the relation can be usedm = ρV; W = mg; area from diameter; rad s⁻¹ from rpm
ModifierA factor multiplies or splits the relationcos θ, sin θ, efficiency, percentage loss, rate × time
StagingThe relation applies in more than one stage or to more than one bodyTwo-leg journey; chain of efficiencies; warm then melt then boil
Hidden constant or conditionA value or state the question never statesg, c, ρ of water, STP, "from rest", "in air", "ideal"
Curveball conceptA principle changes the methodPerpendicular force does no work; Kelvin not Celsius; terminal velocity means a = 0

The Solution Routine

StepActionError it removes
1Write the knowns with units and mark the unknownSolving for the wrong quantity
2Convert everything to SIUnit errors: km h⁻¹, cm², g, minutes, °C
3Name the source relation and the condition that allows itWrong formula: constant a, from rest, ideal

2.1aA car covers 60 km in 1.5 h. Find its average speed in km h⁻¹ and m s⁻¹.v = 60 ÷ 1.5 = 40 km h⁻¹ = 40 ÷ 3.6 = 11.1 m s⁻¹. Ans: 40 km h⁻¹ = 11.1 m s⁻¹

2.2aA car accelerates from rest at 2 m s⁻² for 8 s. Find its final speed and the distance covered.v = u + at = 0 + 2 × 8 = 16 m s⁻¹. s = ½at² = ½ × 2 × 8² = 64 m. Ans: 16 m s⁻¹; 64 m
2.2bA car moving at 30 m s⁻¹ brakes uniformly at 5 m s⁻². Find the stopping distance and the stopping time.v² = u² − 2as with v = 0: s = u² ÷ 2a = 900 ÷ 10 = 90 m. t = u ÷ a = 6 s. Ans: 90 m; 6 s

2.3 Free fall
2.3aA stone is dropped from a cliff and hits the ground after 3 s. Find the height of the cliff and the speed on impact.h = ½gt² = ½ × 10 × 3² = 45 m. v = gt = 10 × 3 = 30 m s⁻¹. Ans: 45 m; 30 m s⁻¹
2.3bA ball is thrown vertically upward at 25 m s⁻¹. Find the greatest height and the total time in the air.H = u² ÷ 2g = 625 ÷ 20 = 31.25 m. Time up = u ÷ g = 2.5 s; total = 5 s. Ans: 31.25 m; 5 s

2.4 Velocity–time graphs

2.5 Projectile motion
2.5aA ball is kicked horizontally at 15 m s⁻¹ from a cliff 20 m high. Find the time to land and the horizontal distance.Vertical: h = ½gt², so t = √(2h ÷ g) = √(40 ÷ 10) = 2 s. Horizontal: x = ut = 15 × 2 = 30 m. Ans: 2 s; 30 m

2.6 Circular motion
