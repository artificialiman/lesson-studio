3. Entry requirements

Standard set (MBA, MPA, MPH, M.CSM, M.SCM, M.ISD, and the MIT programme page)

  • Bachelor's degree in any field, minimum Second Class Lower Division (2:2).
  • HND in any field, minimum Upper Credit, "may be considered".
  • Lower results need a Postgraduate Diploma (PGD). Without a PGD: at least 3 years of work experience.
  • Five O'Level credit passes.

Variations

  • MPH, Epidemiology track: health-related degree at 2:2 or better; HND holders need a health-related PGD; O'Levels must include English, Mathematics, Biology, Physics and Chemistry.
  • MIT home-page FAQ: relevant bachelor's with minimum 2.5 CGPA, or PGD with minimum 3.0 CGPA, or at least two years of relevant work experience. The same FAQ also says two years of post-bachelor's experience is required.
  • MPA legacy tuition page: minimum 2 years of post-bachelor's experience; longer for lower results.
  • M.CSM, M.SCM, M.ISD FAQs: a degree in a related field is "typically expected"; work experience is not mandatory.
  • International applicants: same academic rules; proof of English proficiency may be requested.
  • No application fee (stated on the MIT page). Miva's admissions page says an acceptance letter arrives by email within 24 hours for eligible applicants. One third-party guide reports no entrance examination for master's programmes.

4. Delivery, assessment, support

  • Study level label on the MBA, MPA, MPH, M.CSM, M.SCM and M.ISD pages: "Blended Learning". The MIT, M.CSM, M.SCM and M.ISD FAQs say "fully online".
  • Examinations are online; the MIT FAQ states there are no in-person exams. Miva's undergraduate FAQ states in-person attendance is not compulsory.
  • MIT learning inputs: video lessons, case studies, simulations, projects, masterclasses, workshops. Assessment: assignments, projects, quizzes, final examinations.
  • Tools: virtual classroom on Miva's learning management system (class.miva.university), e-library, Career Advancement Centre, assigned Success Advisors.

7. Intakes and deadlines

  • Three cohorts a year: applications open February for May classes; applications open May for September classes; applications open October for January classes (January 2027 next).
  • September 2026 cohort: programme pages state "apply before 30th September 2026". The M.CSM, M.SCM and M.ISD pages also carry an earlier 31 May 2026 date for the May cohort.
  • Discount applies to full-year (upfront) payment. Monthly instalments are offered on the MIT page and in a third-party guide.
