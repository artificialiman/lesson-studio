Section C — Classification & Behaviour

19.Mechanical: needs medium (sound); EM: no medium (light)
20.Particle motion ⊥ vs ∥; crests vs compressions; polarisable vs not
21.Transverse; oscillation is perpendicular to travel, so can be filtered to a single plane

25.Progressive transfers energy; standing stores energy with fixed nodes and antinodes

Answer Key — Behaviour & Calculation

Section A — Reflection, Refraction, Diffraction

1.θi = θr (both from the normal)
2.Speed changes with medium; f constant; v = fλ forces λ to change → direction changes
3.v decreases to 2×10⁸ m s⁻¹; f unchanged; λ decreases
4.n = 1.5
5.Gap size ≈ wavelength
6.Strong diffraction — waves spread out in semicircles
7.Sound λ is comparable to doorway width; light λ (~500 nm) is far smaller, so diffraction is negligible
8.n = c/v

Section B — Interference & Superposition

9.Resultant displacement = vector sum of individual displacements
10.Constructive: Δd = nλ; destructive: Δd = (n+½)λ
11.Constructive (Δd = 2λ = nλ, n = 2)
12.Destructive (Δd = 3.5λ = (3+½)λ)
13.Superposition: crests reinforce (loud), crest+trough cancel (quiet)
14.2A
15.0 (complete cancellation)
16.Constant phase difference; without it, pattern shifts and averages out

Section C — Standing Waves & Wave Function

17.Two identical waves travelling in opposite directions superpose
18.Node: zero displacement; antinode: maximum displacement
19.Node-to-node = λ/2; node-to-antinode = λ/4
20.λ = 2L = 2.4 m
21.λ = L = 1.2 m
22.λ = 0.8 m
23.A = 0.02 m, k = 6 rad m⁻¹, ω = 300 rad s⁻¹
24.λ = 2π/6 ≈ 1.05 m; f = 300/2π ≈ 47.7 Hz; v = fλ ≈ 50 m s⁻¹
25.Progressive transfers energy; standing stores it (net transfer = 0)
