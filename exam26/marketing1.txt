SECTION A
Choose the correct answer from options A–D.
1.	Marketing can best be defined as the process of ______.
A. producing goods only
B. identifying and satisfying customers’ needs profitably
C. selling goods at high prices
D. transporting goods from one place to another

2.	Which of the following is a nature of marketing?
A. It is customer-oriented
B. It is only concerned with production
C. It does not involve exchange
D. It discourages competition

3.	The scope of marketing includes all of the following except ______.
A. buying and selling
B. transportation
C. promotion
D. punishment of consumers

4.	One major importance of marketing to a business is that it ______.
A. helps to identify customers’ needs
B. prevents production
C. eliminates competition completely
D. stops communication with customers

5.	Marketing is important to society because it ______.
A. creates awareness of available products
B. prevents people from buying goods
C. reduces employment opportunities
D. discourages production

6.	The production concept assumes that consumers will prefer products that are ______.
A. expensive and scarce
B. available and affordable
C. heavily advertised
D. imported only

7.	The product concept focuses mainly on ______.

B. reducing the number of customers
C. avoiding innovation
D. increasing transportation costs

8.	The selling concept emphasizes ______.
A. aggressive selling and promotion
B. customer satisfaction only
C. reducing production
D. avoiding advertising

9.	The marketing concept focuses on ______.
A. producing without considering consumers
B. satisfying customers’ needs better than competitors
C. selling at any cost
D. increasing prices

10.	The societal marketing concept considers the interests of ______.
A. the producer only
B. the seller only
C. consumers, the business and society
D. government alone

11.	Which of the following is an example of a consumer good?
A. A sewing machine bought by a garment factory
B. A refrigerator bought by a household
C. Industrial machinery
D. Factory equipment

12.	Industrial goods are goods purchased mainly for ______.
A. personal consumption
B. further production or business use
C. decoration of homes
D. entertainment

13.	Which of the following is a consumer good?
A. Raw materials bought by a factory
B. Office equipment bought by a company
C. Toothpaste bought for personal use
D. Industrial machinery

14.	Durable goods are products that ______.
A. can be used for a long period
B. are consumed immediately
C. cannot be stored
D. are always cheap

15.	Which of the following is a non-durable good?
A. Television
B. Refrigerator
C. Bread
D. Furniture

16.	Which of the following is an example of a service?
A. Hairdressing
B. School uniform
C. Bread
D. Chair

17.	Services are generally described as ______.
A. tangible
B. intangible
C. durable goods
D. raw materials

A. Product, Price, Place and Promotion
B. Product, People, Profit and Production
C. Price, People, Place and Profit

19.	Which element of the marketing mix deals with what a business offers to customers?
A. Price
B. Place
C. Product
D. Promotion

A. place
B. price
C. promotion
D. product

21.	In the marketing mix, Place mainly refers to ______.

A. Advertising
