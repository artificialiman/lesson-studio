SECTION A
Instruction: choose the options that best answers each question.
1.	A company introducing a newly developed product will most likely use informative advertising at the initial stage because it seeks to ______.
A. compare its product with competitors
B. create awareness and provide information about the product
C. discourage consumers from buying competing products
D. persuade existing customers to change brands

2.	A detergent manufacturer states in an advertisement that its product removes stains better than Brand X. This is an example of ______ advertising.
A. informative
B. persuasive
C. competitive
D. institutional

3.	A major reason why manufacturers advertise their products is to ______.
A. create demand and increase sales
B. eliminate all forms of competition
C. prevent consumers from comparing products
D. reduce the need for distribution

4.	Which of the following combinations contains only advertising media?
A. Radio, television and billboard
B. Warehouse, newspaper and insurance
C. Bank, radio and transport
D. Factory, magazine and cinema

5.	One major disadvantage of advertising to a consumer is that it may ______.
A. provide information about alternative products
B. encourage the purchase of goods that are not really needed
C. increase competition among producers
D. improve the quality of products

6.	Which factor is most likely to reduce the effectiveness of advertising in Nigeria?
A. Wide availability of advertising media
B. Illiteracy among part of the target audience
C. Increased competition among producers
D. Improved communication technology

7.	Which of the following is most likely to encourage the development of tourism in Nigeria?
A. Poor road networks
B. Political instability
C. Improved security and transportation
D. Destruction of tourist attractions

8.	A foreign tourist visiting Nigeria and spending money on accommodation, food and transportation contributes directly to ______.
A. capital flight
B. foreign exchange earnings
C. reduction in employment
D. trade restriction

9.	Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
A. Olumo Rock — Ogun State
B. Yankari Game Reserve — Lagos State
C. Obudu Mountain Resort — Kano State
D. Erin-Ijesha Waterfall — Bauchi State

10.	The development of tourist centres can contribute to economic development mainly by ______.
A. creating employment and generating income
B. reducing domestic investment
C. discouraging cultural exchange
D. reducing government revenue

11.	A businessman insures his warehouse against fire. If the warehouse is destroyed by fire, the insurance company will compensate him subject to the terms of the policy. This illustrates the principle of ______.
A. utmost good faith
B. indemnity
C. contribution
D. subrogation

12.	An insured person deliberately fails to disclose that a building has previously suffered serious fire damage. Which principle of insurance has been violated?
A. Indemnity
B. Contribution
C. Utmost good faith
D. Proximate cause

13.	A person cannot insure a property in which he has no financial interest because this would violate the principle of ______.
A. insurable interest
B. contribution
C. indemnity
D. subrogation

14.	A trader has insured the same stock with two different insurance companies. If a loss occurs, the principle that prevents him from receiving more than the actual loss is ______.
A. contribution
B. utmost good faith
C. insurable interest
D. proximate cause

15.	Which of the following best explains the importance of insurance to business?
A. It guarantees that no loss will occur
B. It transfers certain financial risks from the business to the insurer
C. It eliminates competition in the market
D. It guarantees business profitability

16.	Which type of insurance would be most appropriate for a company transporting goods by sea?
A. Fire insurance
B. Marine insurance
C. Life assurance
D. Motor insurance

17.	A manufacturer obtains a loan from a commercial bank to purchase additional machinery. The bank is performing its function of ______.
A. accepting deposits
B. granting credit
C. issuing currency
D. regulating the economy

18.	Which banking service would be most useful to a business that regularly makes payments to suppliers in different parts of Nigeria?
A. Electronic funds transfer
B. Safe custody
C. Savings account only
D. Foreign tourism

19.	Development banks differ from ordinary commercial banks mainly because development banks ______.
A. concentrate on financing projects that promote economic development
B. accept deposits only from government employees
C. provide only short-term consumer loans
D. are established mainly to advertise businesses

20.	A large company seeking assistance with the arrangement of long-term finance and other corporate financial services would most likely approach a ______.
A. microfinance bank
B. merchant bank
C. commercial bank only
D. savings bank

21.	The major importance of microfinance banks to the Nigerian economy is that they ______.
A. provide financial services to small businesses and low-income earners
B. finance only multinational corporations
C. control the country’s monetary policy
D. issue Nigeria’s currency

22.	Which of the following is the best example of how banks contribute to economic growth?
A. Mobilising savings and channeling funds into productive investment
B. Discouraging people from saving
C. Preventing businesses from obtaining credit
D. Reducing investment opportunities

23.	When a commercial bank grants loans, it can contribute to economic development by ______.
A. increasing productive activities and employment
B. reducing the supply of goods
C. preventing entrepreneurship
D. eliminating foreign trade

24.	A manufacturer produces large quantities of goods during the period of low demand and stores them until demand increases. The warehouse is performing the function of ______.
A. price determination
B. storage and stabilisation of supply
C. production
D. advertising

25.	A Nigerian importer stores goods in a bonded warehouse before paying customs duties. The major advantage is that ______.
A. customs duties can be paid before the goods arrive
B. payment of customs duties is deferred until the goods are released
C. the importer is permanently exempted from customs duties
D. the goods become the property of the warehouse owner

26.	A company storing frozen fish would most appropriately use a ______ warehouse.
A. bonded
B. private
C. cold-storage
D. public

27.	Which of the following best explains how warehousing creates time utility?
A. It changes the form of goods
B. It makes goods available when they are needed
C. It transfers ownership to consumers
D. It reduces the selling price of goods

28.	A trader who does not own a warehouse but wants to store goods temporarily for a fee would most likely use a ______ warehouse.
A. private
B. public
C. bonded
D. government

29.	A warehouse warrant is useful to a trader because it can serve as ______.
A. evidence of ownership of goods stored in a warehouse
B. a document for advertising goods
C. proof that customs duty has been cancelled
D. a replacement for an insurance policy

30.	Which of the following combinations correctly shows the importance of warehousing to commerce?
A. Storage, preservation of goods and continuous supply
B. Production, taxation and currency issue
C. Advertising, insurance and banking regulation
D. Manufacturing, consumption and import licensing
