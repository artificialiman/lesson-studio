Topic 6 · Quadratic equations (factorising)A rectangular stall at the fair is 3 m longer than it is wide, and its floor area is 40 m². What is the width of the stall?
  1. A. 8
  2. B. 5
  3. C. 7
  4. D. 6
Answer: B. 5
Translate: let the width be x m, so the length is (x + 3) m and x(x + 3) = 40. Solve: x² + 3x − 40 = 0, which factorises as (x + 8)(x − 5) = 0, so x = −8 or x = 5. A width cannot be negative, so x = 5. Check: 5 × 8 = 40 ✓.

Topic 7 · Quadratics (completing the square)The profit from the fair's ticket sales, in thousands of naira, is P = −x² + 8x − 7, where x is the ticket price in thousands of naira. What is the maximum profit, in thousands of naira?
  1. A. 4
  2. B. 9
  3. C. 16
  4. D. 10
Answer: B. 9
Translate: the maximum of a downward-opening quadratic occurs at its turning point. Solve: P = −(x² − 8x) − 7 = −[(x − 4)² − 16] − 7 = −(x − 4)² + 9. The squared term is never positive, so P is greatest when x = 4, giving P = 9. Check: P(4) = −16 + 32 − 7 = 9 ✓.
